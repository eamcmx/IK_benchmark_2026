# IK_benchmark_2026: inverse kinematics solvers under warm-started trajectory tracking

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)

MATLAB code and per-cell results of a benchmark of **41 inverse kinematics (IK) solvers**: two baselines
defined by the benchmark and 39 methods implemented from their source papers, selected through a
PRISMA-guided survey. Every solver runs on **six serial arms** (UR5, ABB IRB120, Franka Panda and three
regular redundant arms of 9, 11 and 13 DOF) and **four 100-waypoint trajectories** (rectangle, circle,
lissajous and a workspace-boundary path), warm-started from the solution at the preceding waypoint.

- **Budget:** 2000 forward-kinematics (FK) evaluations per waypoint, counted by the harness, not by the
  solver. Jacobian evaluations are counted and reported, not budgeted.
- **Pose gate:** a waypoint is solved when the position error is below 1e-5 m and the rotation error below
  1e-5 rad, both recomputed from the FK of the returned joints.
- **Two phases:** Phase 1 runs each method as published (its paper's operators, population and fitness);
  Phase 2 follows every solver with the same damped-least-squares polish (at most 50 iterations,
  lambda = 1e-2), skipped when the returned joints already meet the gate.
- A solver whose derivation does not apply to a robot reports the robot as not applicable; those cells are
  shown as n/a and never scored as failures.

## Article

E. A. Merchán-Cruz, G. Aragon-Camarasa, E. Gromova, S. Moveh, *A Comparative Benchmark of Inverse Kinematics Solvers for
Serial and Redundant Manipulators (6 to 13 DOF) under Warm-Started Recursive Trajectory Tracking*, 2026.
Supplementary material (pseudocode of every solver as run): Zenodo, DOI to be added.

## Quick start

```matlab
setup_paths          % adds the folders below to the MATLAB path
demo_quickstart      % UR5, 20 waypoints: two baselines, PINV, QuIK, and WOA in both phases
```

## Layout

| Folder / file | Contents |
|---|---|
| `solvers/` | One `solve_<NAME>.m` per solver, interface `[q, info] = solve_X(robot, T_d, q0, opts)`. Each header gives the full citation of the source paper, the parameters with their pages, the benchmark interface (budget, warm start, units, joint limits) and every deviation from the paper. `default_opts.m` holds the shared options. |
| `bench/bench_run.m` | The harness: runs a solver along a trajectory, counts FK and Jacobian calls, enforces the budget, recomputes the pose error and the gate. |
| `utils/` | FK and Jacobian (`fk_chain`, `jac_geometric`, counted), pose error, the Phase-2 polish (`solve_polished`, `dls_polish`), budget helpers (`ikb_budget_left`, `ikb_generations`). |
| `robots/`, `trajectories/` | The six robot models (DH parameters, joint limits) and the trajectory generators. |
| `run_benchmark.m`, `run_all.m` | The full grid, Phase 1 then Phase 2, into `results/phase1.csv` and `results/polished.csv` (resume-safe; several hours on a laptop). |
| `figures_pipeline/` | Tables, figures and rank statistics from the CSVs (`gen_results.m`). |
| `run_manip_traces.m`, `plot_robot_renders.m`, `plot_trajectories_fig.m` | The manipulability traces, robot renders and trajectory figure (written to `results/figures/`). |
| `tests/test_harness.m` | Checks of the harness: FK and Jacobian counters against an independent count, wall-clock timing, budget flag, not-applicable handling and the polish accounting. |
| `results/` | Per-cell results of both phases and the generated tables and figures (see `results/README.md`). |
| `prisma/` | The literature search behind the solver selection (see below). |

## Requirements

MATLAB (tested with R2026a). The solvers and the harness use base MATLAB only, except `solve_SQP.m`, which
calls `fmincon` (Optimization Toolbox).

## Reproducing the results

```matlab
setup_paths
run_all                             % 41 solvers x 6 robots x 4 trajectories, Phase 1 then Phase 2
gen_results                         % tables, figures and rank statistics
```

Each cell is seeded deterministically from its solver, robot and trajectory names, so stochastic solvers
reproduce their rows. Wall times depend on the machine; success, errors and FK counts do not.

## PRISMA material (`prisma/`)

`PRISMA_search_log.txt` records every database query (verbatim search strings, dates, record counts), the
eligibility criteria and the selection flow (372 records identified, 29 duplicates, 343 screened).
`screening_decisions.csv` gives the per-record decision with its trigger and rationale, title and DOI;
`flow_counts.txt` the flow counts; `screen.py` and `build_batch_prompt.py` the screening aids; the two
`notebooklm_*` files the logs of the LLM-assisted eligibility check. The raw database exports (Scopus,
IEEE Xplore) and the merged export are not included because they contain publisher abstracts; they can be
regenerated by running the queries in the log.
