ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (set a priori; agreed with author 2026-04-25)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Research question (PICO-style):
  Population : serial robotic manipulators with revolute joints, primarily
               6/7/8 DOF (>= 5 DOF tolerated; hyper-redundant up to ~10 DOF
               kept as supporting context).
  Intervention: a numerical, quasi-Newton, heuristic, or metaheuristic IK
               solver applied to a 6D pose target (or position-only with
               explicit method discussion).
  Comparator : at least one other IK method or explicit baseline.
  Outcome    : any quantitative comparison metric (success rate, accuracy,
               iterations, time, smoothness, manipulability, joint-limit
               clearance, singularity behaviour).

Inclusion criteria (a record must satisfy ALL):
  IC1. Addresses the inverse kinematics problem for a serial revolute
       manipulator.
  IC2. Either (a) compares >= 2 IK methods on a common testbed, or
       (b) proposes a new method and benchmarks it against >= 1 prior
       method. Cross-family comparison NOT required (intra-family
       comparisons such as PSO vs GA vs ABC qualify).
  IC3. Reports at least one quantitative comparison metric.
  IC4. Manipulator has >= 5 DOF.
  IC5. Peer-reviewed journal article OR conference paper indexed in
       Scopus or IEEE Xplore.
  IC6. Published 2005-01-01 or later.
  IC7. English language.

Exclusion criteria (any one disqualifies):
  EC1. Pure analytical / closed-form IK with no comparison or benchmarking.
  EC2. Inverse statics, differential kinematics, or trajectory optimisation
       only - no IK position/orientation problem solved.
  EC3. Cable-driven, continuum, soft, or parallel-only robots (kept only
       if the paper also benchmarks serial-arm IK methods).
  EC4. Human pose estimation, animation rigging, or 3D mesh reconstruction.
  EC5. Editorials, errata, abstracts-only, withdrawn / retracted papers,
       books, theses.
  EC6. Duplicate of a record already included from another database (kept
       once, both source IDs noted).
  EC7. No abstract available in the export AND title insufficient to
       assess relevance.

Primary screening: title + abstract.
Full-text screening (subsequent stage): methodology section.
